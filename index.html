All your email,
kept on your iPhone.

MailCave brings Gmail, iCloud Mail and your work email together in one calm inbox for iPhone and iPad. There are no MailCave servers: your mail goes straight from your provider to your device.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone and iPad

Every account, one inbox

Gmail and Google Workspace, iCloud Mail, and work addresses on your own domain. MailCave finds the settings for you.

Private by design

Mail is stored on your device in an encrypted database. Passwords live in the iOS Keychain. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Sign in with Google

Add Gmail with Google’s own sign-in page and your passkey. No app passwords to copy.

Write with confidence

Name suggestions from your mail and Contacts, a check for forgotten attachments, Send Later and reminders when nobody replies.

Smart, on device

Summaries and sorting use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, so your email isn’t sent anywhere to be read.

Restore from iCloud

Your accounts and settings can be kept in your own private iCloud, ready for your next iPhone or iPad.
